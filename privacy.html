Privacy Policy

Carrot Patrol · Effective 20 November 2026

Carrot Patrol is made by Naor Melamed ("we"). This policy explains what happens to information when you or your child play Carrot Patrol on iPhone, iPad or Android. The short version: we do not collect any personal information, the game has no accounts, and the ads in the free version are set up for children.

Information we collect

None. We do not run servers, the game has no sign-in, and it sends no information to us. We do not use analytics or tracking tools.

Information kept on your device

The game saves your progress (stars and best scores per level), your music and sound settings, and whether ads have been removed, in a file on your own device. This file never leaves the device, and deleting the app deletes it.

Ads

The free version shows a full-screen ad between levels at most once every two levels, never during play. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. Because Carrot Patrol is made for children, every ad request is marked as directed to children (in line with the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act, COPPA) and as coming from a user under the age of consent, and ads are limited to the "G" (general audiences) content rating. This means:

  • Ads are not personalised and are not based on interests or past behaviour.
  • The game does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA on Apple devices, advertising ID on Android) and does not ask for permission to track.
  • Google receives technical information it needs to show an ad and to prevent fraud, such as the device type, operating system, app version, a coarse location derived from the IP address, and which ads were shown or tapped. Google handles this information under its own policies (How Google uses information from apps that use its services, AdMob and child-directed apps). We do not receive information that identifies you or your child.

Remove ads purchase

Ads can be removed with a one-time in-app purchase. To keep children from buying by accident, the purchase button sits behind a short grown-up check. Payment is handled entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) under their own privacy policies; we never see payment or account details. The game only stores, on the device, that ads are removed. You can restore the purchase on a new device from the same Apple or Google account.

Children

Carrot Patrol is made for children aged 4 and up and their families. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). Links and purchases inside the game are kept behind the grown-up check. Parents with questions can contact us at the address below.

App stores

Carrot Patrol is downloaded from Apple's App Store or Google Play. Apple and Google may collect information about downloads, purchases or crashes under their own privacy policies (Apple, Google), and depending on your device settings may share anonymous crash reports with developers. We do not receive anything that identifies you.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Carrot Patrol uses data in a new way, we will update this policy before that version is released and change the date at the top.
