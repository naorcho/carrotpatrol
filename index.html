Quick, silly, satisfying bonks

Cheeky groundhogs pop out of their holes to steal your carrots. Tap them before they get away! Keep at least one carrot safe until the timer runs out, and you win the level. Made for kids from 4 and fun for the whole family: big buttons, bright colours and no reading needed.

15 garden levelsEach one adds something new: bigger gardens, rings, zig-zags and the giant 24-hole Grand Garden.
Different goalsSurvive the timer, bonk a number of groundhogs, keep every carrot safe, or build a combo.
Tricky crittersHelmet groundhogs need two bonks. Golden twins only give up when you tap both at once!
Collect the starsEarn up to 3 stars per level, unlock the next one, and come back to beat your best.
