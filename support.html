Support

Found a bug, have an idea, or need help? Email naorcho@gmail.com. We read every message and usually answer within a few days.

It helps to mention your device model and what happened.

How do I play?

Groundhogs pop out of the holes and try to run off with your carrots. Tap them to bonk them back down. If one gets away, it takes a carrot. Keep at least one carrot until the timer runs out to win the level.

What are the different groundhogs?

A plain groundhog needs one tap. A groundhog wearing a helmet needs two: the first knocks the helmet off. Golden twins pop up together and only give up when you tap both at the same time with two fingers. If you tap just one, it giggles at you!

What do the stars mean?

Finishing a level's goal gives one star and unlocks the next level. Score higher (or finish faster on bonk and combo levels) for two or three stars.

How do I turn the music or sounds off?

Use the note and speaker buttons at the top of the main menu, or on the pause screen (the pause button is at the top right while you play).

How do I remove the ads?

Ads can be removed with a one-time purchase from the main menu, after a short grown-up check. Already bought it on another device? Use "Restore purchase" in the same place, signed in with the same Apple or Google account.

Does the game collect data?

We collect no personal data and the game has no accounts. The free version's ads are child-directed and not personalised. See the privacy policy.
